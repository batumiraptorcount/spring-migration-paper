**THE DESCRIPTIONS BELOW ARE VALID FOR BOTH THE SPRING AND AUTUMN DATA**

**Annual totals per distance zone (filtered):**

- **englishname**: English name of the raptor species.

- **latinname**: Latin (scientific) name of the raptor species.

- **speciesgroup**: Corresponding species group.

- **year**: Year of counting.

- **distance**: Distance code of the observation, indicating the estimated distance between the bird(s) and the observation platform. For a detailed explanation of the distance codes, see Wehrmann et al. (2019): https://zookeys.pensoft.net/article/29252/.

- **yrtot**: Total number of birds counted for the corresponding species, distance code, and year.

- **yr.maxday**: Number of birds counted on the peak day (i.e., the day with the highest total) for the corresponding species and year.

- **yrtot.back**: Total number of birds counted flying in the opposite seasonal direction — south during spring and north during autumn — for the corresponding species, distance code, and year.

- **yrtot.fin**: Final annual total for the corresponding species, distance code, and year, after subtracting yrtot.back from yrtot.


**Daily totals per distance zone (filtered):**

- **englishname**: English name of the raptor species.

- **latinname**: Latin (scientific) name of the raptor species.

- **speciesgroup**: Corresponding species group.

- **year**: Year of counting.

- **yday**: Day of the year.

- **date**: Calendar date, including year.

- **distance**: Distance code of the observation, indicating the estimated distance between the bird(s) and the observation platform. For a detailed explanation of the distance codes, see Wehrmann et al. (2019): https://zookeys.pensoft.net/article/29252/.

- **daytot**: Daily total of the corresponding species and distance code.

- **daytot.back**: Daily total of birds flying in the opposite seasonal direction — south during spring and north during autumn — for the corresponding species and distance code.

- **daytot.fin**: Final daily total for the corresponding species and distance code, after subtracting daytot.back from daytot.

**BEWARE**

Whereas a post-hoc correction is normally performed on our autumn data to estimate the proportions of species among unidentified birds on each day (Wehrmann et al. 2019, Vansteelant et al. 2020), this is not the case here. Due to the higher number of unidentified raptors compared to identified raptors in our spring data, we were unable to develop an elegant correction for the daily totals in our spring data. Therefore, both the autumn and spring data provided here contain the cleaned, but uncorrected, daily/annual counts for both spring and autumn.
